# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers and engineering leaders evaluating Sanjay's scope, judgment, technical depth, and evidence of shipped work.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, memorable engineering profile that lets a visitor quickly understand Sanjay's capabilities, explore his work history, and inspect real projects on GitHub and the App Store.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, and approachable. The site should feel engineered with care without becoming cold or overly corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic resume templates, decorative SaaS dashboards, inflated personal metrics, and layouts that over-index on a single current role.

## Design Principles

- Lead with identity and breadth, then let detailed evidence support it.
- Make real work easy to inspect through direct, prominent project links.
- Use strong editorial hierarchy without sacrificing technical clarity.
- Keep interactions purposeful, fast, and understandable.
- Prefer factual specificity over promotional language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
